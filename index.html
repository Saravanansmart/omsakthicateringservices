Our Story

A Family Kitchen, Serving Joy Since 2007

It started in a small family kitchen in Chennai with one belief we still cook by: food made with devotion turns an ordinary day into a memory people talk about for years. Nearly two decades and a thousand-plus celebrations later, that belief hasn't changed — we've just grown big enough to bring it to your table too.

Behind every event is a team that treats your guest list like our own. Grandmother's recipes, spotless kitchens, and staff who show up early and smile through the rush — so you can actually enjoy your own celebration instead of worrying about the food.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

The things most people ask us before booking. Still curious? Just call or WhatsApp.

Which areas do you provide catering services in?

We're based in Chennai and cater right across all of Tamil Nadu — every district and town — for weddings, receptions, corporate events, house functions and festivals. Wherever your celebration is in the state, we'll bring the feast to you.

How many guests can you cater for?

From intimate house gatherings of 25 guests to grand weddings and corporate events of 2,000+, we scale the kitchen, staff and setup to match your headcount — without ever cutting corners on taste.

Can the menu be customised to my budget and taste?

Always. Every menu is built around your occasion, cuisine preferences and budget — pure-veg, non-veg, Jain, traditional South Indian sadhya, live counters and more. We'll even arrange a tasting session before you decide.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and large events, 2–4 weeks' notice is ideal, but we do our best with last-minute requests too. The earlier you reach out, the more date and menu options you'll have to choose from.

Do you provide serving staff, crockery and setup?

Yes — we offer full-service catering: trained serving staff, crockery, live counters, buffet setup and clean-up afterwards. You just enjoy the celebration; we handle the rest.

Planning a celebration? Let's make it delicious.

Tell us your date and guest count — we'll send a free, tailored quote within one business day. No pressure, just good food.
