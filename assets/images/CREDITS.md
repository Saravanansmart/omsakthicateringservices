Creative Commons licences. Every CC BY / CC BY-SA image **must be attributed** wherever it is
displayed — that is what `/credits.html` (linked from the site footer) does. Keep that page in
sync if any image here is added, replaced or removed.

Files were downloaded from the "Original file" link on each Commons page, converted to JPEG
